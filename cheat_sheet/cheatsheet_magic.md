# Quick Trick
## Cheat Key
- **earse** - to erase the selected area 
- **earse [layer name]** - to erase particular layer (eg. erase metal1)
- **move [direction] [value]** - to move selected region (eg. move up 10 , move right 40)
- **label [name]** - to label region
- **paint [layer name]** - to paint layer (eg. paint nwell)
- **v** - fit layout in the middle
- **z** - zoom in
- **Z** - zoom out
- **f** - flip horizontal
- **F** - flip vertical
- **s** - select topmost cell
- **a** - select all layers
- **u** - undo
- **U** - redo
- **b** - cell box details
